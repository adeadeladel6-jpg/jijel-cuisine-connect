# Jijel Flavor Hub

​Act as an expert Web Designer and Full-Stack Web Developer. Design and construct a modern, high-converting, fully responsive website for a top-tier restaurant located in Larbi Ben M'hidi, Jijel, Algeria.

​** Restaurant Details & Information:**

​Location: Larbi Ben M'hidi, Jijel, Algeria (حي العربي بن مهيدي، جيجل، الجزائر)

​Phone Number: +213 655 70 09 50

​Opening Hours:

​Saturday – Thursday: 10:59 AM – 12:00 AM

​Friday: 10:59 AM – 01:00 AM

​Key Features & Services: Dine-in, Takeout, Table Reservations, Family & Group friendly, Wheelchair accessible restrooms, Cash-only payment.

​Vibe & Atmosphere: Casual, cozy, warm, and perfect for lunch, dinner, and desserts.

​** Structure & Pages Needed:**

​Hero Section (Landing Page):

​High-resolution food/dining background with dark overlay for clear text contrast.

​Welcoming headline and sub-headline highlighting authentic taste and cozy family atmosphere in Jijel.

​Primary Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons: "View Menu", "Reserve a Table", "Order / Call Now (+213 655 70 09 50)".

​About Us Section:

​Story-driven overview highlighting the comfortable ambiance, family-friendly environment, and passion for serving high-quality lunch, dinner, and desserts.

​Interactive Menu Page/Section:

​Filterable categories: Starters/Appetizers, Main Dishes (Lunch & Dinner), Desserts, Cold & Hot Beverages.

​Food cards displaying item photos, descriptions, price place-holders in DZD (Algerian Dinar), and tags for popular items.

​Table Reservation System:

​User-friendly booking form requiring: Name, Phone Number, Date, Time, Number of Guests, and Special Requests.

​Instant confirmation notice reminding users that payment is Cash-Only upon arrival.

​Location, Working Hours & Contact Section:

​Embedded Google Map pointing to the location in Larbi Ben M'hidi, Jijel.

​Clean display of operating hours (highlighting Friday extended hours).

​Direct click-to-call phone link.

​Footer:

​Quick links, full address, phone number, working hours summary, and social media icons.

​** UI/UX & Technical Specifications:**

​Language & Direction: Primary language in Arabic with RTL (Right-to-Left) layout support, plus option to switch to French.

​Design Style: Warm, modern, appetizing color palette (deep burgundy/warm amber/gold accents with clean, high-contrast dark or light background).

​Responsiveness: Mobile-first design, fast loading speed, and seamless navigation across all devices.
